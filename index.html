Good questions.

Can I order ahead?

Yes. Tap + next to anything on the menu, choose a pickup time and pay at the counter when you collect. We'll text you when it's ready.

Do you have gluten free options?

Yes. Our pastries are gluten free and baked in house. Please ask at the counter about anything else.

What milks do you have?

Whole milk, organic half and half, and oat, pistachio, almond and cashew. Non dairy and half and half are 1.00 extra.

Is there anything vegan?

A vegan hot cocoa, plus sorbetto in the gelato case on most days. Ask what's in today.

Do I need a reservation?

No, it's walk in. For a larger group in the evening, give us a call first so we can make room.

Do you check ID?

Yes. Alcohol is served to guests 21 and over with a valid ID.

Hear about new flavours first.

Today's case, new specials and events, about twice a month.
